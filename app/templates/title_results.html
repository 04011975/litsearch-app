Title Search

Search for scientific publications by title across multiple scholarly sources.

Title matches are classified as exact (identical title), normalized (equivalent after text normalization), or partial (the search title occurs as a consecutive sequence of words within a longer title).

Search limitations: Semantic Scholar uses a general search followed by local title matching. Its results may therefore omit relevant title matches even when the source is available. Title matching is limited to the candidate records retrieved from the connected sources.
